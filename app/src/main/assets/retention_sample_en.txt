A small travel checklist

Before leaving, save the address of your accommodation and a local emergency contact. Keep a copy of your booking available offline.

Carry a refillable water bottle. Check the weather and bring comfortable shoes. Leave some room in your plan for a rest and an unexpected discovery.

At a new place, a greeting and a thank you are useful beginnings. Ask before taking someone's photograph and follow the local guidance.
